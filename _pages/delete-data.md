## Delete journal records and photos {#records}

Open the relevant brew, coffee or equipment record in BeanZen, choose its delete action, and confirm the deletion. Remove individual photos from the record's photo controls where available.

Deleting an item removes it from BeanZen's local journal. It does not delete copies you previously saved to Photos, exported, shared, or backed up.

## Clear saved Hoto scale tokens {#tokens}

On the brewing screen, **press and hold RESET** to clear the saved Hoto tokens. A regular tap resets the brew; the long press clears saved tokens. Do this **before uninstalling**, because iOS Keychain items can survive app removal.

Clearing a token means you may need to repeat Xiaomi setup before connecting to that scale. It does not delete your Xiaomi account or remove data held by Xiaomi. To manage that account, use Xiaomi's account controls.

## Remove all local app data {#all-data}

Before starting, export any records you want to keep and check the exported files. This action can permanently remove your local history.

- **Android:** open your device's Settings, find **Apps → BeanZen → Storage** (wording varies), and choose **Clear storage / Clear data**. You can also uninstall the app. Clear saved Hoto tokens first.
- **iOS:** clear saved Hoto tokens in BeanZen first. Then open **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → BeanZen → Delete App**. Choose **Delete App**, not **Offload App**, because offloading preserves app data.

Menu names depend on your operating-system version. These actions remove application data on that device. They do not remove separate exports, saved images, backups, or information held by a third-party service.

## Remove exported files, photos and backups {#copies}

Delete copies separately from the Files or Photos app, your chosen sharing destination, and any device backup service. Check recently deleted folders if you want to remove those copies permanently. A backup restore can bring back previously saved app data.

BeanZen has no developer-operated server holding a copy of your journal, so there is no remote BeanZen account deletion request to submit.

## Request deletion of support correspondence {#contact}

Email **[{{ site.email_address }}](mailto:{{ site.email_address }}?subject=BeanZen%20data%20deletion%20request)** with the subject **BeanZen data deletion request**. Identify the support conversation you want removed, preferably from the address you used to contact us. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify the request.

We delete the support information we hold when it is no longer needed to handle your request and related follow-up, unless we must keep it by law. We will explain any applicable retention requirement in our reply. The developer cannot remotely erase records stored only on your device.

For help with local deletion, use the same email address. No BeanZen login or app reinstallation is required to contact us. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ prefix | append: '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for more detail.
